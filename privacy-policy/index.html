Privacy Policy for the Sluxer App

Effective Date: 21 October 2026 (replaces the version of 11.08.2025)

Controller

Sluxer Polska sp. z o.o., ul. Piaseczyńska 54, 00-765 Warsaw, Poland, KRS 0001177532, NIP 5214120394. Contact: cyber@sluxer.com.

What the app processes

  • Account data – your e-mail address and password (we store only a one-way hash of the password), and the list of devices linked to your account.
  • Device data – measurements, the operating plan, settings and technical data of your Sluxer devices, which the app shows and lets you change. What these are, and how long we keep them, is described in the Sluxer devices and services privacy policy.
  • Camera – used only to scan the QR code on the device label. No photos or videos are recorded or stored. The code holds the device's serial number and its access credentials: the app uses them to connect to the device over Bluetooth, and sends the serial number and a one-time claim code to the Sluxer cloud to link the device to your account. The cloud stores only a hash of the claim code.
  • Bluetooth – to configure the device during installation (WiFi network, installer password, battery pairing). This connection is encrypted.

Why

To provide the service you use the app for: your account, access to your devices, and controlling and monitoring your installation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and to keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Recipients and where data is stored

Hosting providers acting on our behalf, as listed in the devices and services privacy policy (section 6). Account and device data are stored in the European Economic Area.

Your control

  • You can revoke camera and Bluetooth permissions at any time in your phone settings; without them QR scanning and device configuration will not be available.
  • You can delete your account in the app; see account deletion.
  • You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl) or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will publish changes here with a new effective date.

Jakie dane przetwarza aplikacja

  • Dane konta – adres e-mail i hasło (przechowujemy tylko jego jednokierunkowy skrót) oraz lista urządzeń przypisanych do konta.
