Account & Data Deletion – Sluxer

You can delete your account yourself in the Sluxer application: use the delete-account option and confirm with your password. The account and your access to devices are removed immediately.

To have your account deleted for you, or to have the measurements of your device deleted as well, write to us:

Request account and data deletion (cyber@sluxer.com)
